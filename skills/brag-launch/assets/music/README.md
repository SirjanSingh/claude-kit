# Music

No tracks are bundled in this copy of the skill.

The upstream skill shipped five "Happy Beats / Business Moves" tracks from
[ende.app](https://ende.app/en) and generated cue presets for them. Upstream's own notes say to
verify the license before redistributing, and no license text is published alongside the files,
so they were left out.

To use music, drop your own track into the composition (see `references/audio.md`, "Music in
this copy") and generate cues with `scripts/analyze_music_cues.py` or `hyperframes beats`.
